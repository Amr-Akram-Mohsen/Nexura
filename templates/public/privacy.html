Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026. Your privacy and trust are fundamental to how we build Nexura.

1. Privacy by Design

At Nexura, we believe technology news should be accessible without pervasive surveillance. We do not sell your personal data to data brokers, nor do we run invasive third-party tracking scripts that follow you across the web.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address, display name, and securely hashed password.
  • Library & Interactions: Articles you bookmark, videos you save, and topics you follow to power your personal library.
  • Aggregated Telemetry: Anonymized view counts and topic momentum metrics to curate trending discovery feeds.
  • Technical Logs: IP addresses and user-agent strings temporarily processed for rate limiting, bot protection, and server security.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

Nexura uses essential first-party cookies and local storage tokens for:

  • Authentication: Secure session cookies when you log into your account.
  • Security: Cryptographic CSRF tokens protecting form submissions against cross-site forgery.
  • Theme Preferences: Storing your preferred light or dark mode choice locally in your browser.

4. Your Rights & Data Control

Regardless of your jurisdiction, you have full control over your personal data:

  • You can inspect and update your profile details at any time from your account settings.
  • You can request a complete export of your saved library and activity data.
  • You can request the permanent deletion of your account and associated records by contacting us.

5. Contact Our Privacy Desk

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection desk at privacy@nexura.tech.
